Building a granny flat in Delacombe

Delacombe is a south-western suburb with later family homes and newer streets.

Newer homes here can sit on smaller lots with estate covenants or easements, so check the lot size and any estate rules early.

Post-war and 1980s homes often sit towards the front of the block, leaving a backyard that may suit a detached granny flat.

Rules and approvals

Delacombe is in the City of Ballarat area. In Victoria a small second dwelling of up to 80 m² usually will not need a planning permit in a residential zone if the lot is at least 300 m², but a building permit is still required. See our VIC approvals guide.
